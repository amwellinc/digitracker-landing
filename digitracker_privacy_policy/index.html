This Privacy Policy explains how DIGI5Y ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use DIGITRACKER — our remote-workforce management platform — whether accessed directly at digitracker-app.digi5y.co, embedded within the Digi5y AI-CRM, or connected to GoHighLevel through our Marketplace App integration. By using DIGITRACKER in any of these contexts, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who We Are

DIGITRACKER is developed and operated by DIGI5Y. DIGI5Y also builds and operates the Digi5y AI-CRM, our customer-relationship-management platform used by agencies and businesses to run marketing, sales, and client-communication workflows. DIGITRACKER is offered both as a standalone product and as a workforce-management module made available to Digi5y AI-CRM customers — this policy governs your data in either case.

2. Information We Collect

Account & workspace data

Name, work email, role, phone number, country, reporting schedule, department, and other profile fields you or your administrator provide when creating a DIGITRACKER account or sub-account.

Work activity data

Clock in/out times, idle and lunch status, periodic screen captures (where enabled by your administrator), task and KPI submissions, leave requests and supporting documents, payroll records entered by your administrator, and internal message-board posts.

Billing data

Company name, billing email, plan, and subscription status. Card details are collected and processed directly by our payment processor (Stripe) — we never receive or store full card numbers ourselves.

GoHighLevel / Digi5y AI-CRM integration data

When an administrator connects DIGITRACKER to a GHL sub-account (including a Digi5y AI-CRM workspace, which runs on GoHighLevel infrastructure), we receive and store: the location ID, an OAuth access token and refresh token (used only to maintain the connection — never shown to any user), and contact records (name, email, phone) sent to us via webhook events. We request the following scopes, each limited strictly to the integration features described in DIGITRACKER's Settings page: contacts.readonly, contacts.write, locations.readonly, and users.readonly.

3. Use Within Digi5y AI-CRM

Digi5y AI-CRM customers can enable DIGITRACKER as an integrated workforce-management module for their own team and their agency's clients. When DIGITRACKER is used this way:

  • Your Digi5y AI-CRM contacts can be mirrored into DIGITRACKER as potential team members, using the same GHL integration scopes described above.
  • Time-tracking, task, KPI, and leave data created in DIGITRACKER remains scoped to your workspace and is not merged into, or made searchable from, unrelated Digi5y AI-CRM accounts.
  • Authentication is handled by DIGITRACKER directly (Supabase Auth); the Digi5y AI-CRM does not receive your DIGITRACKER password or session tokens.
  • Disconnecting the integration, from either product, stops all further data flow between them going forward.

In short: the Digi5y AI-CRM is DIGI5Y's own CRM product, built on GoHighLevel. When you use DIGITRACKER inside it, the same protections in this policy apply — the CRM relationship does not grant broader access to your workforce data than a standalone GHL Marketplace connection would.

4. How We Use Information

  • To operate core DIGITRACKER features: time tracking, task and KPI management, leave management, HR document storage, and payroll records.
  • To mirror GHL / Digi5y AI-CRM contacts as potential team members inside your DIGITRACKER workspace, when the integration is connected.
  • To keep your GHL connection active (OAuth token refresh) and to reflect install/uninstall events automatically.
  • To notify workspace Admins and Super-Admins of account and subscription events (e.g. new signups, leave requests, payment failures).
  • To process subscription payments and send order confirmations and invoices.
  • To provide customer support and diagnose technical issues.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your data — from DIGITRACKER, GHL, or the Digi5y AI-CRM — for third-party advertising.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis (e.g. GDPR), we process personal data to perform our contract with your organization (providing the DIGITRACKER service), to comply with legal obligations (e.g. payroll and tax records), and on the basis of legitimate interests such as securing our platform and improving our products. Where consent is the applicable basis — for example, optional integrations — you may withdraw it at any time as described in Section 13.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your data. We share information only with:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf under contract — Supabase (database, authentication, file storage) and Stripe (payment processing).
  • GoHighLevel / Digi5y AI-CRM, strictly to the extent needed to operate an integration you or your administrator explicitly connected.
  • Your own organization's administrators, who can see workforce data for their own sub-account by design — this is core to how DIGITRACKER functions.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, only where required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of DIGI5Y, our users, or the public.
  • A successor entity, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets — subject to this policy continuing to apply to your data.

7. Data Isolation Between Workspaces

DIGITRACKER is multi-tenant: each company ("sub-account") is technically isolated from every other, including sub-accounts connected through the Digi5y AI-CRM. Row-level security enforced at the database layer ensures a workspace's data — including its GHL/AI-CRM connection, contacts, payroll, and leave records — is never visible to another workspace.

8. Data Retention

We retain account and work-activity data for as long as your workspace remains active, plus a reasonable period afterward for legal, tax, and accounting purposes. Screen captures are automatically purged after 30 days. If a GHL/AI-CRM integration is disconnected, we delete the stored OAuth tokens immediately; previously synced contact records remain until deleted by your administrator. If your workspace (sub-account) is deleted by an administrator, associated user accounts and their work-activity data are permanently removed, with the exception of financial transaction records and HR-offboarding archive records, which we retain for legal and accounting purposes.

9. Security

OAuth tokens and other credentials are stored encrypted at rest and are never exposed to the browser — all privileged operations (token exchange, refresh, and disconnect) run through server-side functions using a scoped service role. Access to any workspace's data requires authentication and is enforced by row-level security policies scoped to that workspace. All traffic between your browser and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

10. Cookies & Tracking

This marketing site (digitracker.digi5y.co) does not set analytics or advertising cookies. The DIGITRACKER application (digitracker-app.digi5y.co) uses only the minimum storage necessary to keep you signed in — a session token managed by our authentication provider — and does not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

11. International Data Transfers

Our infrastructure providers (Supabase, Stripe) may process and store data in regions outside your own country. Where required, we rely on those providers' standard contractual safeguards for cross-border data transfers.

12. Children's Privacy

DIGITRACKER is a workplace tool intended for use by employed adults and is not directed at, or knowingly used to collect information from, children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us using the details in Section 15 and we will remove it.

13. Your Choices & Rights

  • Workspace Admins can disconnect the GHL / Digi5y AI-CRM integration at any time from Settings, which immediately revokes our stored access.
  • Uninstalling DIGITRACKER from your GHL Marketplace or Digi5y AI-CRM account automatically removes our stored tokens for that location.
  • Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or request deletion of your personal data.
  • You may request export or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the address below; your workspace administrator can also action most requests directly within the product.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page; where a change materially reduces your rights, we will take reasonable steps to notify affected workspace Admins.

15. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to admin@digi5y.co.
